# NR-UI-011

Derived from baseline requirement `UI-011` (Keyboard, accessibility and iPad usability). Each obligation is carried verbatim as the criterion that bears its id.

| ID | Statement |
|---|---|
| UI-011-O1 | Support keyboard-only navigation, visible focus, skip-to-content, appropriate landmarks, semantic status announcements, labeled controls, non-color-only states, and minimum usable touch targets. |
| UI-011-O2 | Support iPad portrait/landscape, narrow mobile screens, desktop, 200% text reflow, reduced motion, and screen readers. |
| UI-011-O3 | No essential operation may require drag-and-drop or hover. |
| UI-011-O4 | Preserve focus and scroll context during transitions and WebAssembly handoff. |
| UI-011-O5 | Graphs require equivalent accessible textual interaction. |
