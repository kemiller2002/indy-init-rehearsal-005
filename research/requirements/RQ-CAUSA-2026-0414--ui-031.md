Derived from baseline requirement `UI-031` (Preserve system detail without exposing it in the workflow). Each obligation is carried verbatim as the criterion that bears its id.

| ID | Statement |
|---|---|
| UI-031-O1 | Moving information out of investigation views MUST NOT delete operational observability or evidence provenance. |

| UI-031-O4 | Settings MUST contain only supported user-adjustable options; read-only technical facts belong in Administration. |
| UI-031-O5 | Apply role/access controls to sensitive operational information and never expose credentials. |
