# NR-ADRP-013

Derived from baseline requirement `ADRP-013` (Decision escalation). Each obligation is carried verbatim as the criterion that bears its id.

| ID | Statement |
|---|---|
| ADRP-013-O1 | Agents may decide local naming, refactoring, and visual details within this profile. |
| ADRP-013-O2 | Escalate: new dependency; schema or event changes; domain policy semantics; auth/security shortcuts; breaking interface; cutting P0; new release candidate after a failed gate; changing frozen fixtures. |
| ADRP-013-O3 | Escalations require options, impact, recommendation, and recorded decision. |
| ADRP-013-O4 | Do not poll the coordinator for reversible local details. |
