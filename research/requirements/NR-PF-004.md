# NR-PF-004

Derived from baseline requirement `PF-004` (Contract fidelity). Each obligation is carried verbatim as the criterion that bears its id.

| ID | Statement |
|---|---|

| PF-004-O2 | Maintain a machine-readable fixture manifest with IDs, schema versions, expected domain versions/digests, and expected error outcomes. |
| PF-004-O3 | Do not mutate the frozen historical dataset. |
