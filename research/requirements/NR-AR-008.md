# NR-AR-008

Derived from baseline requirement `AR-008` (Adversarial independent review). Each obligation is carried verbatim as the criterion that bears its id.

| ID | Statement |
|---|---|
| AR-008-O1 | A reviewer distinct from the implementer must attempt to falsify high-risk claims, inspect requirements and evidence, and record gaps. |
| AR-008-O2 | Reviewers cannot self-ratify operator decisions. |
