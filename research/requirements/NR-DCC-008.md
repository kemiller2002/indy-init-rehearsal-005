# NR-DCC-008

Derived from baseline requirement `DCC-008` (Acceptance identity). Each obligation is carried verbatim as the criterion that bears its id.

| ID | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| DCC-008-O1 | A release MUST NOT be called Accepted until the deployed artifact identifies the exact CI-verified SHA and the deployment acceptance job for that SHA concludes successfully. |
| DCC-008-O2 | A green local or mocked browser run is insufficient. |
| DCC-008-O3 | Failed, pending, skipped, or unread acceptance jobs cannot be treated as PASS. |
