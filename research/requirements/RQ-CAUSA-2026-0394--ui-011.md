| ID | Statement |
|---|---|
| UI-011-O1 | Support keyboard-only navigation, visible focus, skip-to-content, appropriate landmarks, semantic status announcements, labeled controls, non-color-only states, and minimum usable touch targets. |

| UI-011-O3 | No essential operation may require drag-and-drop or hover. |
