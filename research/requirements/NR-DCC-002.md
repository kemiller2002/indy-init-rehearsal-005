# NR-DCC-002

Derived from baseline requirement `DCC-002` (Early adversarial review). Each obligation is carried verbatim as the criterion that bears its id.

| ID | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| DCC-002-O2 | Review MUST NOT wait for browser or deployment acceptance. |

| ID | Statement |
|---|---|
| DCC-002-O1 | A bounded independent reviewer MUST inspect architectural decisions, interfaces, and critical invariants before the first release candidate, targeting completion by T+90 to T+120 in a seven-hour run. |
| DCC-002-O3 | Review findings must be actionable, reproducible, and assigned to a requirement/invariant. |
