# NR-UI-025

Derived from baseline requirement `UI-025` (Investigation overview as the primary landing view). Each obligation is carried verbatim as the criterion that bears its id.

| ID | Statement |
|---|---|
| UI-025-O1 | Opening an investigation MUST lead to an Overview that answers, from domain-authoritative projections only: what happened and impact; current supported findings; supporting and contradictory evidence; unresolved questions; and next authorized actions. |
| UI-025-O2 | Show unknown/unavailable information explicitly rather than inventing summaries. |
| UI-025-O3 | Overview sections SHOULD link to Summary, Causal Analysis, Evidence, Hypotheses, Timeline, and Corrective Actions. |
| UI-025-O4 | No AI-generated interpretation or unsupported conclusions may be presented as established fact. |
