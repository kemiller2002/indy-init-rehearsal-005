# NR-WDL-007

Derived from baseline requirement `WDL-007` (Responsive and accessible rendering). Each obligation is carried verbatim as the criterion that bears its id.

| ID | Statement |
|---|---|
| WDL-007-O1 | Support desktop, iPad portrait/landscape, and mobile with accessible text equivalents, meaningful labels, keyboard navigation/focus, adequate contrast, zoom/reflow or intentional navigable overflow, and reduced-motion preferences. |
| WDL-007-O2 | Diagram information must not depend on color alone. |
