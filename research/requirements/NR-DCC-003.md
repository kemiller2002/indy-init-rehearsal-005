# NR-DCC-003

Derived from baseline requirement `DCC-003` (Defect-class classification). Each obligation is carried verbatim as the criterion that bears its id.

| ID | Statement |
|---|---|
| DCC-003-O1 | Every critical finding and every recurring major finding MUST record: defect-class ID, minimal reproduction, affected requirements, underlying architectural cause, invariant violated, impacted paths, severity, proposed class-level prevention, owner, and closure evidence. |
| DCC-003-O2 | Ordinary isolated defects may use lighter records. |
| DCC-003-O3 | Multiple manifestations of one cause MUST be linked to the same class. |
